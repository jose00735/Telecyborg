# 12 — Ideas backlog

Ideas that complement the three stages. Each is small enough to slot into a minor release.

| Idea | Why it helps | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Webcam strip mode** — a short, wide always-on-top window that sits right under the webcam | Your eyes stay near the lens on video calls | v0.2 |
| **Hidden from screen share** — Win32 `SetWindowDisplayAffinity(WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE)` | When you share your screen in a customer meeting, the prompter does not appear in the share | v0.2 |
| **Phone remote over LAN** — scan a QR code, your phone becomes play/pause/next | Control without touching the keyboard on camera | v0.2 |
| **Global hotkeys** | Control the prompter while another window (slides, the call) has focus | v0.2 |
| **Rehearsal history chart** — total time and per-paragraph drift across runs | See your pitch tighten over rehearsals | v0.2 |
| **Cheat-card mode** for meetings — bullets and numbers instead of full prose, big and glanceable | Customer meetings need prompts, not reading | v0.3 |
| **Numbers card** — pinned facts (ARR, CSAT, dates) that can be shown on demand | Never round a number under pressure | v0.3 |
| **Multilingual sections** — EN/ES/PT versions of a section with one-key switch | Same pitch in the interviewer's language | v0.3 |
| **Agenda timer** for meetings — sections with time boxes and a gentle "move on" cue | Keeps QBRs on time | v0.3 |
| **Filler-word and silence report** (stage 3) | Concrete feedback after rehearsals | v0.5 |
| **"Answer length" guard** — warns when a Q&A answer runs past 60–90 s | Interview answers stay tight | v0.4 |
| **Post-meeting notes** — export the questions you were asked and the sections you used to Markdown | Follow-ups and better packs next time | v0.4 |
